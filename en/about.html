Our story

A focused appliance repair business serving the GTA

PTS began with a practical goal: make it easier for homeowners to understand an appliance problem and decide what to do next. Every request starts with listening to the symptoms, identifying the appliance and arranging a diagnostic visit.

The business services refrigerators, washers, dryers, electric ovens and stoves, dishwashers, microwaves and range hoods from major brands. PTS serves twelve communities across Toronto and the GTA.
