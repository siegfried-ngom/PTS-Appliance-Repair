Understanding your range hood repair needs

A range hood removes cooking vapour, grease and odours from the kitchen. PTS diagnoses fan, motor, switch, lighting, noise and airflow problems in ducted and recirculating models.

Common problems we diagnose

  • ✓Fan does not start
  • ✓Airflow is weak at every speed
  • ✓Motor hums but the fan does not turn
  • ✓Excessive vibration or rattling
  • ✓Lights do not work
  • ✓Controls or speed selector are unresponsive
  • ✓Grease or odours remain after cooking
  • ✓Fan runs continuously and will not switch off

Appliance types we service

  • Under-cabinet range hoods
  • Wall-mounted chimney hoods
  • Island range hoods
  • Built-in hood inserts
  • Ducted range hoods
  • Recirculating range hoods
Safety first

Disconnect power before removing filters or touching a damaged light socket. A grease-covered filter can restrict airflow; clean or replace filters according to the manufacturer’s instructions, but do not reach into a moving fan.

Repair or replace?

A diagnosis supports a better decision

Switches, lights, capacitors, fan wheels and some motors can often be serviced. Damaged housings, unavailable controls, poor duct design or an aging low-cost unit may favour replacement or ventilation improvements.
