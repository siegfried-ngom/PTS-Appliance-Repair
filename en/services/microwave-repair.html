Understanding your microwave repair needs

Microwave faults involve high-voltage components that can remain hazardous even after the appliance is unplugged. PTS assesses heating, turntable, door-switch, control and ventilation concerns without unsafe DIY disassembly.

Common problems we diagnose

  • ✓Microwave runs but food stays cold
  • ✓Unit will not start or power on
  • ✓Turntable does not rotate
  • ✓Door will not latch or close
  • ✓Display or keypad does not respond
  • ✓Unusual buzzing or arcing inside
  • ✓Interior light or exhaust fan fails
  • ✓Microwave stops during operation

Appliance types we service

  • Countertop microwaves
  • Over-the-range microwaves
  • Built-in microwaves
  • Microwave drawers
  • Combination microwave ovens
  • Compact microwave units
Safety first

Do not remove the cabinet or attempt internal microwave repairs. High-voltage components can retain a dangerous charge after unplugging. Stop using a unit that sparks, smokes, has a damaged door or operates with the door open.

Repair or replace?

A diagnosis supports a better decision

Door switches, turntable components, controls and fans may be repairable. Damage to the cavity or door, major high-voltage failures, age, installation style and replacement cost influence the decision.
