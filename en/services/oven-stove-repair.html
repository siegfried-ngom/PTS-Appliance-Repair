Understanding your oven & stove repair needs

Uneven temperatures, failed burners and ignition problems make meal preparation difficult and may create safety concerns. PTS diagnoses electric and gas ovens, cooktops and ranges, then explains the repair before work begins.

Common problems we diagnose

  • ✓Oven does not heat or preheat
  • ✓Temperature differs from the selected setting
  • ✓Bake or broil element does not glow
  • ✓Gas burner clicks but does not ignite
  • ✓Flame is weak, uneven or discoloured
  • ✓Electric surface element stays cold
  • ✓Control panel, display or timer is unresponsive
  • ✓Door does not close or seal correctly

Appliance types we service

  • Electric ranges
  • Gas ranges
  • Freestanding stoves
  • Slide-in ranges
  • Built-in wall ovens
  • Electric and gas cooktops
Safety first

If you smell gas, do not ignite a flame or operate electrical switches nearby. Leave the area and contact the gas utility or emergency services from a safe location. Do not use an oven that sparks or overheats.

Repair or replace?

A diagnosis supports a better decision

Elements, igniters, sensors, switches and door components are often replaceable. Control boards, extensive wiring, structural damage, age and parts availability affect the repair-versus-replace decision.
