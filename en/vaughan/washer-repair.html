Understanding your washer repair needs

Washer faults can interrupt a household quickly and leaks may damage nearby flooring. PTS checks drainage, filling, spinning, suspension, door-lock and electronic control systems before recommending work.

Common problems we diagnose

  • ✓Washer will not start or power on
  • ✓Tub does not fill or fills very slowly
  • ✓Water does not drain after the cycle
  • ✓Drum will not spin or clothes remain soaked
  • ✓Machine shakes, walks or becomes unbalanced
  • ✓Water leaks from the door, hoses or underneath
  • ✓Door or lid remains locked
  • ✓Error code appears or cycle stops unexpectedly

Appliance types we service

  • Front-load washers
  • Top-load washers
  • Stackable washers
  • Compact washers
  • Washer-dryer combinations
  • High-efficiency washers
Safety first

Stop using a washer that is leaking, smells burned or moves violently. Shut off its water supply when a leak continues and avoid standing in water near an electrical outlet.

Repair or replace?

A diagnosis supports a better decision

Bearing or tub work can be substantial, while pumps, valves, belts, switches and many controls may be practical to repair. The diagnosis, appliance age and part availability guide the decision.
